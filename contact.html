How do I buy Vyoma?

Vyoma is launching with stores and studios across Dallas–Fort Worth. Until our stockist list is live, send us a note through this form and we’ll help you order or point you to a store near you.

What’s the difference between the 5-Pack and the 10-Pack?

The 5-Pack ($10) has one box of each of our five fragrances. The 10-Pack Starter Kit ($21) has two of each, plus a small incense holder and a ritual page that walks you through the evening ritual.

Is incense safe to burn indoors?

Burning incense produces smoke, like a candle. Burn it in a well-ventilated room, in a heat-resistant holder, and never leave it unattended or burning while you sleep. If you have asthma or another breathing condition, ventilate well or skip the burn. Full directions and warnings are printed on every box.
