Vyoma Wellness · The App

The Nightly Ritual, Guided

An eight-minute evening wind-down of scent, breath and sound, built around your Vyoma fragrance. Coming soon for iPhone and Android.

Why a Ritual

Scent Marks the Moment

Evenings rarely end on their own. A ritual gives them an edge: the same fragrance, the same few minutes of breath and sound, the same signal that the day is done.

Vyoma Wellness turns that into a simple nightly practice. Choose tonight’s fragrance, follow a short guided session, and close the day the same way each night.

The Ritual

Four Steps, Every Evening

01

About 45 min before bed

Prepare

Light one stick of tonight’s fragrance in a ventilated room, and let it burn while you settle in.

02

8 minutes

Wind Down

Follow a guided session of breath and sound, paired with the fragrance you chose.

03

Before lights out

Extinguish

The app asks you to confirm the incense is fully out before you sleep. Every night, no exceptions.

04

Morning

Reflect

A one-tap check-in on how you rested, so you can see which rituals suit you best.
