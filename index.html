An evening ritual in four steps

The same fragrance, the same few minutes, the same signal that the day is done.

About 45 minutes before bed

Prepare

Light one stick of tonight’s fragrance in a ventilated room and let it burn while you settle in.

Eight minutes

Wind down

Follow a guided session of breath and sound, paired with the fragrance you chose.

Before lights out

Extinguish

Confirm the incense is fully out before you sleep. Every night, no exceptions.

In the morning

Reflect

A one-tap check-in on how you rested, so you learn which rituals suit you.
