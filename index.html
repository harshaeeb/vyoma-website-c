Our Story

Scent for the Space
Between Breaths

Vyoma — Sanskrit for “infinite space” — was born from a conviction that the most profound fragrances on earth belong in every home, every practice, every morning ritual.

We partner with artisan families in Bangalore’s Malleswaram district, where the craft of hand-rolling agarbatti has been passed down for generations. No charcoal binders. No synthetic fillers. Just pure botanicals, resins, and sacred intention.
