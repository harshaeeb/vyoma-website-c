Shipping Policy

← Back to Vyoma

Effective date: June 1, 2026 · Last updated: June 7, 2026

We ship from our Dallas–Fort Worth fulfillment partner. Most orders are processed within 1–2 business days.

1. Domestic Shipping (Continental US)

Method Estimated Delivery Cost
Standard 5–7 business days $4.99 (free over $35)
Expedited 2–3 business days $9.99
Overnight Next business day $19.99

2. Wholesale / B2B Shipping

Wholesale orders (50+ units) qualify for free standard shipping. Expedited options available at cost. Large orders may ship freight — contact b2b@vyoma.com for a freight quote.

3. Order Processing

Orders placed before 12pm CST on business days are typically processed same day. Orders placed after 12pm or on weekends/holidays are processed the next business day. You will receive a tracking number by email when your order ships.

4. DFW Local Orders

For yoga studios, temples, and businesses in the Dallas–Fort Worth metro, we offer direct delivery for wholesale orders over $200. Contact b2b@vyoma.com to arrange.

5. International Shipping

We currently ship to the US and Canada. Additional international destinations are coming soon. Contact hello@vyoma.com for international wholesale inquiries.

6. Tracking

All orders include tracking. Check your email for your tracking number, or contact hello@vyoma.com if you don't receive it within 3 business days of ordering.

7. Lost or Delayed Shipments

If your tracking shows no movement for 7+ business days, email hello@vyoma.com. We will investigate and, if the package is confirmed lost, reship or refund your order.

8. Contact

Shipping questions: hello@vyoma.com
