6. Product Safety

Incense involves open flame and produces smoke. Always observe these precautions:

  • Burn on a non-combustible, heat-resistant holder in a well-ventilated space.
  • Never leave burning incense unattended.
  • Keep away from children, pets, and flammable materials.
  • Discontinue use if respiratory discomfort occurs.
  • Store away from moisture and direct sunlight.
