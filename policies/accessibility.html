Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

← Back to Vyoma

Effective date: June 1, 2026 · Last updated: June 7, 2026

Vyoma is committed to ensuring our website is accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We strive to meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards.

1. Our Commitment

We believe that access to information and the ability to shop our products should be available to everyone. We are continually working to improve the accessibility of our website and will implement relevant standards as they evolve.

2. Measures We Take

  • Semantic HTML with appropriate heading hierarchy
  • Alt text on all meaningful images
  • Sufficient color contrast ratios (minimum 4.5:1 for body text)
  • Keyboard navigable interface
  • Focus indicators on all interactive elements
  • ARIA labels on navigation, forms, and modals
  • No reliance on color alone to convey information

3. Known Limitations

We are aware of the following areas where accessibility improvements are in progress:

  • Some older PDF documents may not be fully screen-reader compatible — text versions available on request
  • Video content (coming soon) will include captions and audio descriptions

4. Third-Party Content

Our site may include third-party content (social media embeds, maps) that we do not control. We cannot guarantee the accessibility of third-party services.

5. Feedback and Contact

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of Vyoma's website. If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need assistance, please contact us:

  • Email: accessibility@vyoma.com
  • We aim to respond to accessibility inquiries within 2 business days

We will work with you to find an accessible alternative or provide the information you need in a format that works for you.

6. Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the US Department of Justice ADA information line at 1-800-514-0301 or visit ada.gov.
