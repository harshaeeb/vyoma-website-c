Our Commitment

Vyoma LLC is committed to ensuring vyomaincense.com is accessible to the widest possible audience, including people with disabilities. We actively work to improve accessibility in accordance with WCAG 2.1, published by the W3C.

Conformance Standards

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA across our website, covering the four core principles:

  • Perceivable: information must be presentable to all users.
  • Operable: UI must be operable by all users, including keyboard-only.
  • Understandable: content and operation must be understandable.
  • Robust: content must be reliably interpreted by assistive technologies.

We evaluate using automated tools, manual keyboard navigation, and screen reader testing (NVDA, VoiceOver).

Accessibility Features

  • Semantic HTML5 landmarks (nav, main, footer, section, article) for screen reader navigation.
  • All images include meaningful alt text; decorative images use alt="".
  • Colour contrast ratios meet WCAG 2.1 AA minimums (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text).
  • All interactive elements are keyboard-focusable with visible focus indicators.
  • Form inputs are paired with visible labels; required fields are clearly indicated.
  • Cookie consent dialog uses role="dialog" and aria-label.
  • Page language declared (lang="en") to assist screen reader pronunciation.
  • No content relies solely on colour to convey meaning.
  • No content flashes more than three times per second.
  • Text resizes up to 200% without loss of content or functionality.

Known Limitations

  • Base64 logo images: may not deliver alt text in all screen reader modes. We are evaluating solutions.
  • Third-party content: payment processor iframes may not fully conform to our standards. We work with vendors to improve this.
  • PDF documents: some linked PDFs may not be fully accessible. We are working on HTML alternatives.

If you encounter a barrier not listed here, please let us know.

Feedback & Contact

We welcome feedback on accessibility barriers, alternative format requests, and improvement suggestions.

  • Email: accessibility@vyomaincense.com
  • General: hello@vyomaincense.com

We aim to respond within 5 business days.

Need help completing a purchase due to a disability? Email accessibility@vyomaincense.com — a team member will assist you directly.

Formal Complaints

If unsatisfied with our response, you may contact:

  • United States: US Department of Justice, Civil Rights Division (ADA complaints).
  • European Union: Your national accessibility authority under the EU Web Accessibility Directive.
  • United Kingdom: The Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC).

We always prefer to resolve concerns directly before formal proceedings.
