1. Who We Are

Vyoma LLC (“Vyoma,” “we,” “us”) operates vyomaincense.com and related services. We are headquartered in Dallas, Texas, USA. For GDPR purposes, Vyoma LLC is the data controller.

2. Data We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account & order data: name, email, shipping address, phone number, and payment details (processed via PCI-compliant third parties; we do not store raw card numbers).
  • Contact form submissions: name, email, message content, and inquiry type.
  • Wholesale applications: business name, contact name, address, EIN, and references.
  • Newsletter & marketing: email address and preferences.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Log data: IP address, browser type, OS, referring URL, pages visited, and timestamps.
  • Cookies & similar technologies: see Section 5 for details.
  • Device information: screen resolution, device type, and language settings.

Information from Third Parties

We may receive data from payment processors (transaction status), shipping carriers (delivery confirmation), and analytics platforms (aggregated usage data).

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Processing and fulfilling orders and communicating about them.
  • Responding to inquiries, support requests, and wholesale applications.
  • Sending transactional emails (order confirmation, shipping updates).
  • Sending marketing emails only if you have opted in; you can opt out at any time.
  • Improving our website through aggregated analytics.
  • Complying with legal obligations (tax reporting, fraud prevention).
  • Enforcing our Terms & Conditions and protecting against misuse.

Legal basis (GDPR): contractual necessity (orders), legitimate interests (fraud prevention, analytics), consent (marketing emails, non-essential cookies), and legal obligation (tax/accounting).

4. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers: payment processors, shipping carriers, email service providers, and analytics platforms — only as needed to provide their service.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of Vyoma or others.
  • Business transfers: in the event of a merger or acquisition, your data may transfer to the successor entity subject to equivalent privacy protections.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Essential Cookies

Required for the site to function (shopping cart, session management). Always active.

Analytics Cookies

Privacy-respecting analytics to understand site usage. Only set after you click “Accept all” in our cookie banner.

Marketing Cookies

We do not currently use advertising or retargeting cookies.

Managing Cookies

Withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser’s local storage (key: vyoma_cookie_consent) and refreshing the page.

6. Data Retention

  • Order data: 7 years (Texas tax record-keeping requirement).
  • Contact form submissions: 2 years, or until resolved.
  • Marketing data: until you unsubscribe, after which a suppression record is retained.
  • Analytics data: 26 months in aggregated/anonymized form.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: request deletion (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Portability: receive your data in a machine-readable format (GDPR / EU residents).
  • Opt-out of sale: we do not sell personal data (CCPA / California; TDPSA / Texas).
  • Non-discrimination: exercising rights will not affect service or pricing.

To exercise any right, email privacy@vyomaincense.com. We respond within 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA/TDPSA) of a verifiable request.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has submitted data to us, email privacy@vyomaincense.com and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

We implement TLS encryption, access controls, and limited data retention to protect your data. No internet transmission is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Material changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact & Data Protection

  • Privacy: privacy@vyomaincense.com
  • General: hello@vyomaincense.com
  • Mailing: Vyoma LLC, Dallas, TX, USA

EU/EEA residents may also lodge a complaint with their national supervisory authority.
