Data & Privacy

Privacy Policy

← Back to Vyoma

Effective date: June 1, 2026 · Last updated: June 7, 2026

Vyoma LLC ("Vyoma," "we," "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website or purchase from us.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Name, email address, phone number (contact forms and orders)
  • Billing and shipping address (orders)
  • Business name and type (wholesale inquiries)
  • Payment information (processed by our payment processor — we do not store card numbers)

Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, pages visited, time on site (analytics)
  • Cookie data (see Section 5 below)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil orders
  • To communicate about your order or inquiry
  • To send marketing emails (with your consent — unsubscribe anytime)
  • To improve our website and products
  • To comply with legal obligations (Texas TDPSA, CCPA where applicable)

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Payment processors (to process transactions)
  • Shipping carriers and 3PL partners (to fulfil orders)
  • Analytics providers (aggregated, anonymized data)
  • Law enforcement (when legally required)

4. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise these rights, email privacy@vyoma.com.

5. Cookies

We use two categories of cookies:

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function (cart, session). Always active.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the site. Only active with your consent.

You can manage cookie preferences via the banner when you first visit, or contact us to update your preferences.

6. Data Retention

We retain order data for 7 years (tax compliance). Marketing preferences and analytics data are retained for 2 years or until you opt out, whichever comes first.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS and encrypted payment processing. No method of transmission is 100% secure — we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Our site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact privacy@vyoma.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy periodically. Material changes will be noted on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@vyoma.com · Vyoma LLC, Dallas, TX
