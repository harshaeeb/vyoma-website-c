Returns & Refunds

Refund Policy

← Back to Vyoma

Effective date: June 1, 2026 · Last updated: June 7, 2026

Your satisfaction is sacred to us. If you are not completely happy with your Vyoma purchase, we want to make it right.

1. Consumer Orders — 90-Day Return Window

We accept returns within 90 days of the delivery date for consumer orders (direct website and Amazon orders). To be eligible, products must be:

  • In original, unopened packaging, or
  • Opened but substantially unused (more than 80% of sticks/cones remaining)

2. How to Initiate a Return

  1. Email returns@vyoma.com with your order number and reason for return
  2. We will send a prepaid return shipping label within 2 business days
  3. Ship the item back within 14 days of receiving the label
  4. Refund will be issued within 5 business days of receiving the return

3. Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to a Vyoma error or defective product.

4. Damaged or Defective Products

If your order arrives damaged or defective, email returns@vyoma.com with a photo within 14 days of delivery. We will replace the product or issue a full refund including shipping — no return required.

5. Wholesale Orders

Wholesale orders are subject to a 90-day return window from delivery. Contact b2b@vyoma.com for wholesale return authorizations. Custom label orders are non-refundable.

6. Non-Refundable Items

  • Gift sets where all components have been opened and used
  • Custom label wholesale orders
  • Items purchased from third-party retailers (contact the retailer directly)

7. Exchanges

We are happy to exchange any product for a different fragrance or format within the return window. Email returns@vyoma.com to arrange.

8. Contact

Return questions: returns@vyoma.com
